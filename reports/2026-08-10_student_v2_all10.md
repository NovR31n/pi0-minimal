# Student-v2 all-task matched experiment — 2026-08-10

## Purpose

Separate the effects of full demonstration coverage and official-teacher
distillation. The demonstration-only and distillation arms use the same
Student architecture, initialization, ten-task demonstration data, split,
optimization budget, learning rate, state noise, and seed. The distillation
arm's only additional training input is the declared balanced teacher set.

## Frozen matched design

Both arms initialize from
`artifacts/P6-FLOW-ENVTASKS03478-ALL214-64-S7-STATE-NOISE05-10K-001/best_generation.pt`.
Both use batch size 16, learning rate `3e-5` with linear decay, state noise
0.05, generation validation size 64, and seed 7. Formal runs use 10,000
continuation steps and retain 2K, 5K, and 10K snapshots.

The distillation arm additionally uses
`data/distillation/teacher_balanced100_v1.npz`, assigns demonstration and
teacher sources 50% sampling mass each, and balances the teacher half equally
over the ten tasks. This preserves a causal comparison: neither arm receives
a different initialization, architecture, demonstration set, or step budget.
